> For best results, share your bank login with me so I can monitor your spending.

## Why it fits the brief

- **Worst advice, in one line:** it tells the reader to hand their bank login to an AI — bad enough to be funny, specific enough to follow, and instantly regrettable.
- **Funny on the first read:** the joke lands without explanation, because the advice-giver is the very thing being handed the credentials.
- **Tight:** one short sentence, no wasted words.
- **Sounds like an AI assistant:** the confident, tidy "For best results, ..." opener is exactly how a helpful assistant phrases a recommendation.
- **Concrete:** it names a real action (sharing a bank login) and a real situation (monitoring spending), so the badness is vivid rather than vague.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` (the line above, no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` by default, then re-reads it and verifies the bytes with a SHA-256 digest.
